Movie Webpage Project

Context

We were required to make a movie web project about a fictional movie that would allow us to practice our HTML and CSS concepts with a partner. So, me and my partner decided to do a action movie from a nostalgic show we used to watch when we were younger. The tittle of our movie is the Legendary StickMan Battle.

Content

For our project, we were required to create a plan were we had to decide what the story should be and also divide up the work between us.Where than we decided to write about a stickman battle between two nemisis. To sum up our story, it's basically about a stickman villian (Namkicits) who wants to detroy the city and builds an army, and a Hero who is defending the city. The hero wins, but the outcome of the battle and the saftey of the city had a sacrafice, Him. Our story was meant to be nostalgic and thrilling, sharing a fun moments for anyone my age who veiwed it.

Reflection

Some challenges me and my partner faced while making this project was trying to center images. If I had other padding or margin in the same selector for the image centering, it would interfere and our images wouldn't be centered. From this challenge, I was able to learn how to successfully center images with ease and im happy to know that this project gave me a css skill that I probably wont forget.

Additionally, when creating our plan and picking a story idea. It was really hard to come up with something cool considering the time frame we had, and we were thinking but also procrasinating a lot so we decided to look through old projects that were given to us as examples and used them to help us in the creation of the story. So I would say that from this, I was able to take away that time runs out so you need to set your priorities straight and stop procrasinating .

For my next steps, I would've probably added more coding and creative things to the story but I ran out of time.
